# LEADS

## Leads, warmest first

![leads-01-list.png](leads-01-list.png)

**LEADS** holds the people you're getting to know. Each wears an orb showing
how warm the relationship is, from frozen to supernova, and the list opens on
the follow-ups that need you.

**Add a lead** needs only a name. A high-priority **First interaction** comes
with it, due now unless you pick a time.

## A lead

![leads-03-sheet.png](leads-03-sheet.png)

Log what happens: type it, give it points, press Enter. The suggestions fill
both in. The temperature works itself out from the points and cools in quiet
weeks, or set it yourself on the slider until **Back to automatic**.

Follow-ups say what to do next and when. Tick one done, **Snooze** it, or
**Dismiss** its reminders. They also show on the quick strip, in NEXUS and in
CALENDAR.

## Opportunities

![leads-14-board.png](leads-14-board.png)

When something concrete comes of a lead, press **Make an opportunity**: what it
is, its kind and its value. The lead stays. Opportunities move through **Open**,
**In talks**, **Negotiating**, **Agreed** and **In progress** to **Won** or
**Lost**, with follow-ups of their own.

## Deleting

![leads-17-delete.png](leads-17-delete.png)

**Delete lead**, in red at the foot of a lead's sheet, deletes it with its
history, follow-ups and opportunities; it asks first. **Archive** beside it
keeps everything instead. An opportunity has **Delete opportunity** in the same
place, a follow-up has **Delete** beside **Edit**, and a logged interaction has
a faint **×** at the end of its line.
