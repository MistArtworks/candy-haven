# CONSULTATION

## Share a track

![consultation-02-share.png](consultation-02-share.png)

Press **Share a track…**, then **Choose a file…** or drop a bounce on the
dialog. Any audio file on this computer, up to 200 MB.

Check the title and tempo, choose **1 DAY** to **7 DAYS**, say what to listen
for, and press **Share**. Then **Copy link**, and send it.

## Read and hear their feedback

![consultation-03-feedback.png](consultation-03-feedback.png)

Press **Check for new**, pick the track, then a producer: their page opens.
Their notes are pins and bands on the waveform, listed under it, with the
loudness, scope and stereo meters. Their chain of devices is on the right;
press one to see its settings, the EQ drawn as a curve.

**Play**, or press a note, to hear it through their settings. **As it is** and
**their settings** are the A/B; the transport bar does it from any page.
Switch a device off to hear the track without it, or fold its view away.

## When it closes

A link closes when its days run out, or with **End now**. Then the page stops
working, and the track and all its feedback are deleted. Its name stays,
marked **EXPIRED**, until you **Remove** it.

> Feedback not read before the link closes is gone for good. The rail counts
> what's new.
