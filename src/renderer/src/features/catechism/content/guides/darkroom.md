# DARKROOM

## Develop a photograph

![darkroom-01-plate.png](darkroom-01-plate.png)

Press **Open image**, or drop a photograph onto the plate. It lands on the
console's own palette straight away; everything after that is adjustment.

Hold the pointer down on the plate to see the original, and let go to see the
grade again.

## Tone, curve and ramp

- **TONE**: exposure, the black and white points, gamma and contrast, in the
  order they are applied. Double-click a control to put it back.
- **CURVE**: click the line to add a point, and drag a point off the square to
  remove it.
- **RAMP**: click the bar to add a stop, drag it, and pick its colour from the
  palette.

![darkroom-02-ramp.png](darkroom-02-ramp.png)

## Export

**Export** writes the graded image at full resolution, through the same
arithmetic the plate is drawn with. What you see is what is written.

**Reset grade** starts again from the defaults.
