# SERVICES

## DJ enquiries

What "Book me as a DJ" sends lands here when the console starts, and whenever
you press **Check for new**. Same sign-in as CONTACT.

A new enquiry stays new until you move it to **IN TALKS**, **CONFIRMED** or
**DECLINED**. Having read it is not an answer.
