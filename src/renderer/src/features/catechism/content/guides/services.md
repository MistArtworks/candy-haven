# SERVICES

## DJ enquiries

![services-01-enquiries.png](services-01-enquiries.png)

What "Book me as a DJ" sends lands here when the console starts, and whenever
you press **Check for new**. Same sign-in, and the same check-in, as CONTACT.
The list opens on **OPEN**: what is new or in talks.

## Taking one up

![services-02-enquiry.png](services-02-enquiry.png)

Open it, press **Reply by email**, and move it along: **IN TALKS**, then
**CONFIRMED** or **DECLINED**, and **ARCHIVED** when nothing more will come of
it. A new enquiry stays new until you do; having read it is not an answer. Keep
the details in **Note**. **Delete** removes it from the website too, and asks
first.

> Statuses and notes stay on this machine. The website never sees them.

## Producer bookings

![services-03-producer.png](services-03-producer.png)

Every booking from "Book me as a music producer" is already paid for: half a
commission, or a session in full. It arrives with the same check-in. Open one
for who, when and what they paid; **Copy the pay link** when a commission is
ready for its other half.

## Moving or cancelling a session

![services-08-move.png](services-08-move.png)

**Move to another time**, pick the day and the time, and the website does the
rest: the old time opens up, the calendar invite moves, and the client is
emailed. **Cancel the session** frees the time and tells them, with your note.
Any refund is made in Stripe.

## The services

![services-04-offerings.png](services-04-offerings.png)

OFFERINGS is what the website offers, in its order. **Start from the website's
list** the first time. Edit a service, file it with the unsaved-changes bar,
and press **Publish** over the list when you're ready. Nothing reaches the
website before that.

## The hours

![services-07-hours.png](services-07-hours.png)

HOURS is when sessions can be booked: start times for each day, days off, and
the rules. They go with the next **Publish**.

> The first publish switches the website from its own list, and asks first.
