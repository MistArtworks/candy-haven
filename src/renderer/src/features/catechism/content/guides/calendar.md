# CALENDAR

## Four lenses, one register

![calendar-01-month.png](calendar-01-month.png)

The same entries, read four ways. The lens lives in the URL, so a link can point
at the agenda and the back gesture walks the views rather than leaving the
department.

- **MONTH** — six weeks at once. Where the work falls.
- **WEEK** — seven days against the clock.
- **DAY** — one day, hour by hour.
- **AGENDA** — the register as a list, from here forward.

## A day's list

![calendar-05-day-list.png](calendar-05-day-list.png)

Click a day in MONTH and its list opens first: everything on it, in order.
Click an entry to edit it, tick it done, or open its attachments. **Add an
entry** files a new one on that day; Esc puts the list away.

The day's number opens it in DAY instead.

## Filing an entry

![calendar-04-entry.png](calendar-04-entry.png)

An entry is your own statement that something happens on a date. Nothing here is
derived from a project or a release, and that boundary is deliberate: a delivery
date you wrote down should not move because a project changed stage.

Press **Add an entry** on a day's list, click an hour in WEEK or DAY, or press
**File entry** (Ctrl+N). Give it a title, a time if it has one, and a kind,
then press **File entry**. Attachments and a checklist can come with it, and
the square beside an entry marks it done.

## What you didn't file

![calendar-06-week.png](calendar-06-week.png)

A release date from the DISCOGRAPHY shows on its day in every lens, marked
with a gold diamond rather than a chip. A year after a release goes out, a
hollow diamond marks the same day again as its anniversary.

Follow-ups from ARTISTS' leads are marked at their time with a small arrow:
crimson when overdue, struck through when done. Click one to open its lead.
Both are readings, not entries: change them where they live.
