# REGULATION

## Eleven categories

![regulation-01-categories.png](regulation-01-categories.png)

Settings, grouped rather than laid out all at once. The category lives in the
URL, so a link can point at one.

- **PRESENTATION** — motion, interface scale, and the accent the console draws with.
- **DIRECTORY** — the rail: your own categories, what is pinned to the top, and their order.
- **STARTUP** — what opens at launch, whether the console starts with the machine, and what closing it means.
- **QUICK STRIP** — the strip's look, its pins and groups, and today on it.
- **LEADS** — the interactions you log, how fast a lead cools, and follow-up reminders.
- **ARCHIVE** — where projects are filed, and the local database holding the register.
- **INTEGRATIONS** — accounts and services the broadcast kit and the website inbox read from.
- **BOARD** — the shared database behind DISPATCH.
- **UPDATES** — release channel and how new versions arrive.
- **REHEARSAL** — test mode, for exercising the kit without a live audience.
- **DIAGNOSTICS** — where this installation keeps its files, and what it runs on.

Changes wait in the bar at the foot of the page: **File changes** keeps them,
**Discard** puts them back.

## The rail, your way

![regulation-11-directory.png](regulation-11-directory.png)

**DIRECTORY** arranges the rail: pin the pages you use most to the top, make and
rename categories, and drag pages between them by their handles. Pin a category
to keep it first and open. **Back to the console's own** undoes it all, pins
aside. Page names never change.

## The quick strip

![regulation-06-strip.png](regulation-06-strip.png)

Show it or hide it, choose its look, keep it on top, and set how early
CALENDAR's reminders come. **PINS** lists everything on it: rename, drag to
reorder or into a group, and add stacks, projects, files, folders, websites,
departments and actions.

## Leads

![regulation-08-leads.png](regulation-08-leads.png)

The suggestions offered when logging an interaction, each with its points:
rename, re-point, remove, or type a new one and press Enter. Below them, how
many points a quiet week costs a lead, and **Remind me** for follow-up
notifications.

## Set these first

Two settings gate whole departments, so they are worth doing before anything
else.

1. **The filing root**, chosen at the ARCHIVE's own setup gate. Nothing in the
   ARCHIVE works until it points at a directory; REGULATION → ARCHIVE then
   reports it.
2. **INTEGRATIONS → Spotify and Twitch.** Only needed if you intend to run
   NOW TRANSMITTING, THE CONCORD or THE MUSTER.
