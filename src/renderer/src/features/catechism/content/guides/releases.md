# RELEASES

## DISCOGRAPHY, on the website

![releases-01-list.png](releases-01-list.png)

**Publish everything** sends every release once and switches the website over
for good. After that a release goes when its sheet in DISCOGRAPHY is done with,
with only the fields that changed. Behind the DISPATCH sign-in.

## Pick, then Update

Tick releases and pick **Show**, **Hide**, **Add to shelf**, **Remove from
shelf** or **Send**. Nothing goes until **Update**, which sends it all in one
request; until then each row's tags show what will change. **Discard** drops
it.

## Shown or hidden

A **DRAFT** is hidden until its day; **SCHEDULED** shows before it, with
pre-save links; **RELEASED** shows. Show and Hide on a release not out yet
switch it between the two. On one that's out, Hide keeps it off the website by
hand.

## The shelf, covers and tapes

![releases-02-covers.png](releases-02-covers.png)

Up to eight releases sit on the home page shelf, in the order set in its panel;
with none picked, the website shows the newest eight. Only a release visitors
see can go on it. Covers follow on their own, one at a time, and each becomes
its release's tape on the shelf. The strip at the top says what's going.

> A send that can't reach the website waits. **Sync now** sends everything new,
> changed or waiting, together.
