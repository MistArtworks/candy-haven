# RELEASES

## DISCOGRAPHY, on the website

![releases-01-list.png](releases-01-list.png)

**Publish everything** sends every release once and switches the website over.
After that a release goes when its sheet in DISCOGRAPHY is done with.

## Pick, then Update

Tick releases and pick **Show**, **Hide**, **Add to shelf**, **Remove from
shelf** or **Send**. Nothing goes until **Update**, which sends it all in one
request. **Discard** drops it.

## Shown or hidden

A **DRAFT** is hidden until its day; **SCHEDULED** shows before it, with
pre-save links; **RELEASED** shows. Show and Hide on a release not out yet
switch it between the two.

## Covers and the shelf

![releases-02-covers.png](releases-02-covers.png)

Covers follow on their own, one at a time, and each becomes its release's tape
on the home page shelf. The strip at the top says what's going.
