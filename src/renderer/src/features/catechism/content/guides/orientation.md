# Welcome to Candy Haven

## An operator's console

![practice-01-altar.png](practice-01-altar.png)

Candy Haven is the console for the CANDY HEIST practice — one installation, one
operator. It files your projects, tracks them from an idea to a finished master,
runs the overlays your stream is watching, keeps the dated register of what
is due, and follows up with the people you want to work with.

The remit is deliberately wide. The intent is that everything the practice
involves is filed here, rather than spread across a dozen applications that
cannot see one another.

It is written as a department of an institution rather than as an app, so
everything is numbered, named and filed. That is deliberate, and once you know
the vocabulary the whole console reads the same way.

> This tour shows once. Everything in it is in the CATECHISM at more length,
> and every department's **QUICK GUIDE** button (or `F1`) is always there.

## THE QUICK STRIP: the console, one click away

![strip-01-panel.png](strip-01-panel.png)

The small window you met first. It stays on top of everything from the moment
Haven starts, and steps aside while this console is on screen.

- **Today**, from CALENDAR, with overdue follow-ups leading in red.
- **Pins**: projects, stacks, folders, files, departments and websites, one
  click each. Right-click one for its options, or drop a file on the strip to
  pin it.
- The gear for its settings, **More** for the console and the vestibule, and
  the power button, which quits Haven on a second press.

Closing this console does not quit Haven. The strip carries on.

## The rail is an organisation chart

![welcome-03-rail.png](welcome-03-rail.png)

Departments are grouped into six categories, and the grouping tells you what
each one is for. One category is open at a time, the one you are in; press
another's heading to look inside it. A red mark on a closed one means something
in it is waiting for you.

- **COMMAND**: the overview, the calendar, and instruction.
- **STUDIO**: where the work is made, heard and developed.
- **CATALOGUE**: what is released, and how the website shows it.
- **COMMUNITY**: the people you're getting to know, work with, and hear from.
- **PUBLICATION**: the stream while it is live, and the website's own pages.
- **OVERSIGHT**: the condition of the installation, and the rules it runs under.

**Search pages**, at the top of the rail, finds any department by name or by what it is for (`Ctrl+Shift+F`). `Ctrl+1` through `Ctrl+9`, then `Ctrl+0`, open the first ten in the order the rail draws them, and the numbers match what you can see. REGULATION, under DIRECTORY, rearranges it: your own categories, and pins at the top.

## NEXUS: the front door

![nexus-02-overview.png](nexus-02-overview.png)

The landing states what the installation is and reports whether it is healthy.
The orb is not decoration: it carries the live state of the archive, so a glance
tells you whether the database behind everything else is up. Follow-ups due
today, and any overdue, sit in its top right corner.

Scroll once and the diagnostic grid is underneath — archive state, the boot that
produced this session, runtime versions, the update channel, and the register of
departments.

## ARCHIVE: everything you have made

![welcome-04-archive.png](welcome-04-archive.png)

Two controls do most of the work here, and keeping them apart is what stops the
page becoming a settings screen.

- The **lens** decides what is in scope: the whole register (**ALL**), the
  filing tree (**STACKS**), work found on disk and not yet filed (**INTAKE**),
  the **BIN**, or what was opened lately (**RECENT**).
- The **view** decides how it is drawn: **ICONS**, the **LIST**, or the
  pipeline **BOARD**, where a project's stage is changed by dragging it.

## LEADS: who you might work with

![leads-01-list.png](leads-01-list.png)

ARTISTS is who the practice works with. **LEADS**, first in COMMUNITY, is who
it might: each lead has a temperature, from frozen to supernova, that warms as
you log what happens and cools in quiet weeks. **OPPORTUNITIES** is the work
that came of them, stage by stage.

Follow-ups are what keep a lead moving. The ones due lead the page, and an
overdue one shows on the strip, on NEXUS and in CALENDAR until it is done.

## OBSERVATORY: the broadcast kit

![welcome-05-observatory.png](welcome-05-observatory.png)

Every overlay is served from one local address on its own path. Each is a
separate Browser source in OBS: copy the URL from its card, paste it in, and
switch between them with scenes.

The console and the browser source stay in step, so what you set here is what
the audience sees a moment later.

## AUDITORIUM: the listening room

![welcome-06-auditorium.png](welcome-06-auditorium.png)

One file at a time, played and rendered visible. There is no library here on
purpose — the ARCHIVE catalogues your audio, and a second register of the same
material would immediately disagree with it.

This department answers one question, which is what a file sounds like.

## REGULATION: the rules it runs under

![welcome-07-regulation.png](welcome-07-regulation.png)

Settings, grouped into ten categories. **STARTUP** decides what opens when
Haven starts, **QUICK STRIP** sets the strip's look and pins, **LEADS** sets
how leads cool and remind, and **INTEGRATIONS** holds what the Spotify and
Twitch overlays need.

`Ctrl+,` opens it from anywhere.

## Where to find help

![welcome-08-documentation.png](welcome-08-documentation.png)

Two routes, and they answer different questions.

- **QUICK GUIDE**: the button in any department's masthead. A handful of slides
  on how to use _that_ department. Best when you are already on the page.
- **CATECHISM**: the last department on the rail. The full reference, with
  worked examples, chapter by chapter.

Start with COMMISSIONING in the CATECHISM if you have not set up the filing root
yet. Nothing in the ARCHIVE works until you have.
