# ARCHIVE

## Lenses decide what is in scope

![archive-01-lenses.png](archive-01-lenses.png)

The rail down the left is the lens. It changes _what you are looking at_, not
how it is drawn.

- **ALL** — the whole register, flat.
- **STACKS** — the filing tree you build. Categories, genres and artists, and the
  projects shelved on them. These are real directories on disk.
- **INTAKE** — work found elsewhere on disk that is not in the register yet.
- **BIN** — deleted projects, kept until you empty them.

> Albums, EPs and compilations used to be a VOLUMES lens here. They are
> releases in DISCOGRAPHY now.

## Views decide how it is drawn

![archive-02-board.png](archive-02-board.png)

The toggle beside the panel switches between three presentations of whatever the
lens has in scope.

- **ICONS**: a grid of plates. The default, because a shelf is browsed before
  it is audited.
- **LIST**: the table, when the figures are the point.
- **BOARD**: the pipeline, in columns. Drag a project between columns to change
  its stage; the move _is_ the edit.

## The pipeline

Seven stages, in order, plus one off to the side.

`IDEA` → `SKETCH` → `ARRANGEMENT` → `MIX` → `MASTER` → `TRACK READY` →
`RELEASED`

TRACK READY is the gate into DISCOGRAPHY: only work at that stage or later can
be linked to a track on a release.

RELEASED needs the **final master** named — the file that actually goes out —
and is usually set for you when the release goes out.

`SHELVED` is off-pipeline — parked indefinitely, kept for parts rather than for
release, and never moved by anything you do in the catalogue.

## Temporary projects, for quick jobs

![archive-18-temporary.png](archive-18-temporary.png)

**New project** asks **FILED** or **TEMPORARY** first. FILED is the usual: on a
shelf, and kept. TEMPORARY is for a quick job like audio for a video or a
master rebalanced: the name is optional, nothing is filed, and **Open in Live**
opens it straight away from the TEMPORARY PROJECTS folder.

- A temporary project never appears in the register.
- It goes to the Recycle Bin a day after it was last saved. REGULATION, under
  ARCHIVE, changes that, up to keeping it until you remove it.
- One still open in Live is never taken.

> To keep one, **Save As** in Live somewhere INTAKE reads, and file it from
> INTAKE.

## INTAKE files what is already on disk

![archive-03-intake.png](archive-03-intake.png)

Point INTAKE at a folder and it lists what it finds that the register does not
know about. Pick a shelf, move the work onto it, and it becomes a project.

> Whether intake moves or copies is set in REGULATION, under ARCHIVE. Moving is
> the default, because two copies of a project is how you end up mixing the
> wrong one.

## The dossier

![archive-04-dossier.png](archive-04-dossier.png)

Open a project and you get its record: what you have written about it, what is
in the folder, and the history of every stage it has passed through.

Three tabs. **OVERVIEW** is what you write — stage, credits, tags and notes.
**RECORD** is what is read — the set analysis, what is on disk, and which
release the project went out on. **FILES** is the folder itself.

From TRACK READY, OVERVIEW gains a **FINAL MASTER** panel listing the project's
own bounces as tiles. Press ▶ to hear one, click the one that goes out, and press
**SET AS FINAL MASTER**. Nothing moves: the file stays beside the set that made
it, and clicking its name opens the folder.

> Until it is named, the project cannot reach RELEASED. That is the one thing
> in the pipeline this console insists on: a release nobody recorded the source
> of is the question the archive exists to stop being unanswerable.

## Renaming a project

![archive-12-rename-dialog.png](archive-12-rename-dialog.png)

Press the pencil beside a project's name in its dossier, or right-click it and
choose **Rename**. The dialog shows what changes on disk before anything does:
the folder, and every set named after the project.

Its final master, its release track, CALENDAR attachments and quick strip pins
all follow it. Close the set in Ableton Live first: while Live holds the
project open, nothing is renamed and the dialog says why.
