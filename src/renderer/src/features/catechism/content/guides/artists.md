# ARTISTS

## The roster

![artists-01-roster.png](artists-01-roster.png)

Who the practice works with. One record each: a name, a face, what they do,
and where to find them.

The rail under the heading has three lenses: **ROSTER**, **LEADS** and
**OPPORTUNITIES**. This guide starts with the roster.

## Adding somebody

![artists-02-add.png](artists-02-add.png)

Press **Add an artist** to open a dialog. Only the name is required — the
roles, the real name, the colour and the notes can wait — and **Add** hands
straight over to the record's own sheet, where the picture is the first thing
you set.

## Their record

![artists-03-record.png](artists-03-record.png)

The sheet opens as a dossier, not a form: what they're on, how to reach them,
what you wrote down. **Edit** is the door to the fields, and everything there
saves as you stop typing. There is no save button.

## Not the same as an ARTIST folder

The ARCHIVE's filing tree has an ARTIST folder kind. It is a **shelf** — where
a project's files sit on disk.

This is **who made the work**, which is a different fact. A track can credit
three people while living in one folder, and moving it to another shelf does
not rewrite who made it.

> Credits are held as ids, so renaming somebody updates every project and
> every release at once.

## Crediting somebody

Two places, depending on which end you are at.

- **A project** — its dossier, CREDITS on the OVERVIEW tab, beside TAGS and
  NOTES. All three are your own mark on the record, which is what that tab is
  for.
- **A release** — DISCOGRAPHY's sheet, as **Billed as** or **Featuring**.

Removing somebody strips them from everything crediting them, and says how
many before you commit.

## Leads

![leads-01-list.png](leads-01-list.png)

**LEADS** holds the people you're getting to know. Each wears an orb showing
how warm the relationship is, from frozen to supernova, and the list opens on
the follow-ups that need you.

**Add a lead** needs only a name. A high-priority **First interaction** comes
with it, due now unless you pick a time.

## A lead

![leads-03-sheet.png](leads-03-sheet.png)

Log what happens: type it, give it points, press Enter. The suggestions fill
both in. The temperature works itself out from the points and cools in quiet
weeks, or set it yourself on the slider until **Back to automatic**.

Follow-ups say what to do next and when. Tick one done, **Snooze** it, or
**Dismiss** its reminders. They also show on the quick strip, in NEXUS and in
CALENDAR.

## Opportunities

![leads-14-board.png](leads-14-board.png)

When something concrete comes of a lead, press **Make an opportunity**: what it
is, its kind and its value. The lead stays. Opportunities move through **Open**,
**In talks**, **Negotiating**, **Agreed** and **In progress** to **Won** or
**Lost**, with follow-ups of their own.
