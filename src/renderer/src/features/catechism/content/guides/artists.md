# ARTISTS

## The roster

Who the practice works with. One record each: a name, a face, what they do,
and where to find them.

Press **Add an artist** to open a dialog. Only the name is required — the
roles, the real name, the colour and the notes can wait — and it hands
straight over to the record's own sheet, where the picture is the first thing
you set.

The sheet opens as a dossier, not a form: what they're on, how to reach them,
what you wrote down. **Edit** is the door to the fields, and everything there
saves as you stop typing. There is no save button.

## Not the same as an ARTIST folder

The ARCHIVE's filing tree has an ARTIST folder kind. It is a **shelf** — where
a project's files sit on disk.

This is **who made the work**, which is a different fact. A track can credit
three people while living in one folder, and moving it to another shelf does
not rewrite who made it.

> Credits are held as ids, so renaming somebody updates every project and
> every release at once.

## Crediting somebody

Two places, depending on which end you are at.

- **A project** — its dossier, CREDITS on the OVERVIEW tab, beside TAGS and
  NOTES. All three are your own mark on the record, which is what that tab is
  for.
- **A release** — DISCOGRAPHY's sheet, as **Billed as** or **Featuring**.

Removing somebody strips them from everything crediting them, and says how
many before you commit.
