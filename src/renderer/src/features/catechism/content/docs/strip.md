# QUICK STRIP

A small window that stays on top of everything, from the moment the PC starts:
the logo, today's agenda and follow-ups, whatever you pin to it, who is signed
in, the settings gear, and Quit. The console is one click from it, and most
things don't need the console at all.

It is there while the console isn't: open the console and the strip steps
aside; close it, retire it to the tray or minimise it, and the strip is back
where you left it.

![strip-01-panel.png](strip-01-panel.png)

## Folding it away

Click the logo and the strip folds down to the logo alone, a small square in
its corner, in any of the three looks. Click the logo again and it opens. It
stays folded or open as you left it. Folded, drag it by its thin edge.

## Where it sits

It starts in the bottom right of the main screen. Drag it by any empty part to
anywhere, on any screen; near an edge it lines up with it, and it stays where
you leave it, across restarts. If its screen isn't there next time, it comes
back to the bottom right. **Back to the bottom right** in REGULATION puts it
there too.

## Three looks

| Look         | What it is                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `PANEL`      | A card: Today on top, the pins, then who is signed in, the gear, More and Quit |
| `HORIZONTAL` | One bar: the logo, who is signed in, the pins, today's next thing, the tools   |
| `VERTICAL`   | One column on a screen's edge: the same, top to bottom                         |

![strip-09-horizontal.png](strip-09-horizontal.png)

Chosen in REGULATION, under QUICK STRIP. In the one-line looks, today's next
thing is a chip: press it for the Today list. When a follow-up is overdue, the
chip turns crimson, shows a person mark and says **Follow up:** and who.

## Pins

Anything can be pinned, up to two hundred, in any order:

- **A stack**: a category, a genre or an artist from the ARCHIVE. A click asks
  where to open it: **Open in ARCHIVE** shows the stack in the console, **Open
  in Explorer** opens its folder on disk.
- **A project**: a click asks the same. **Open in Ableton** opens its set,
  **Open in ARCHIVE** its dossier, and **Open its folder** its folder on disk.
- **A folder**: clicking it lists what's in it beside the strip. Step into a
  folder and back; click a file and it opens with whatever Windows opens it
  with, Ableton for a set.
- **A file**: one click opens it.
- **A department**: opens the console there.
- **A website**: opens in your browser. See **Websites** below.
- **An action**: New project, Recent projects, Add to today, Open a website,
  the console, the vestibule.
- **A group**: a pin that holds pins. See **Groups** below.

![strip-03-ways.png](strip-03-ways.png)

Tick **Always do this for this pin** as you choose, and from then on a click
on that pin goes straight there. To have it ask again, right-click it and set
**On click** back to **Ask**. REGULATION sets it for every stack and project
pin too.

To pin a file or folder, **drop it on the strip** from Explorer. To pin a web
page, drag its address or a link from your browser onto the strip.

### A pin's options

Right-click any pin for its options, beside the strip:

- its **name**: type a new one and press Enter
- **On click**, for a stack or a project: **Ask**, or the way it always opens
- the ways it can open, one press each
- **Show in Explorer**, for anything on disk
- **Look for its icon again**, for a website
- **Move earlier** and **Move later**
- **Into a group** (one press for each group you have), **Into a new group**,
  or **Out of** its group, **onto the strip**
- **Remove from the strip**, or from its group

![strip-10-options.png](strip-10-options.png)

REGULATION lists every pin. **Stack…** and **Project…** find one in the
ARCHIVE by name, and departments, links and actions are added there too. Drag
a pin by its handle to move it, or drop files and folders on the list to pin
them where they land.

## Groups

A group is one icon on the strip that drops down the pins inside it: the
projects of an album, the sites you check every morning, the folders of a
studio day. It wears an icon you choose, with a small gold corner saying it
opens more.

![strip-04-group.png](strip-04-group.png)

Click a group and its pins drop down beside it, as icons, with the name of the
one under the pointer below them. Each opens just as it would from the strip;
right-click one for its options. A group holds up to 48 pins, one level deep: a
group can't hold another group.

### How to make a group

1. Right-click a pin and choose **Into a new group**.
2. Name the group, and choose its **Icon**, in the options that open.
3. Fill it, any of these ways:
   - press **+** in its dropdown to add a **website** (type its address and
     press **Add website**), a **File…** or a **Folder…**
   - drop files, folders or links on the group's icon
   - right-click any pin on the strip and choose **Into** the group
   - in REGULATION, drag pins in and out of it, or choose it under **Add to**

Right-click a group to rename it, change its icon, move it, ungroup it
(**Ungroup: its pins back on the strip**) or remove it with everything in it.

## Websites

**Open a website** takes any address, opens it in your browser, or pins it.
It's in the strip's **More** menu, and can be pinned as an action of its own.

![strip-05-website.png](strip-05-website.png)

1. Type the address. `youtube.com` is enough: `https://` is added for you, and
   the full address shows under the field.
2. Press **Open**, or Enter, to open it in your browser.
3. Or choose where to pin it, **THE STRIP** or one of your groups, and press
   **Pin it**.

A pinned site wears **its own icon**, fetched once from the site itself, and a
pin still named after its address takes the site's own name. If a site
couldn't be reached at the time, right-click it and choose **Look for its icon
again**.

## Today

The panel's top row is today: what's next on CALENDAR and when, what follows,
how many things are left, and how many are overdue. Releases out today and
release anniversaries show there too. When a follow-up is overdue, it comes
first, in crimson: **FOLLOW UP** and who, with how many more are late.

Click it for the list.

![strip-02-today.png](strip-02-today.png)

**FOLLOW-UPS** come first: every follow-up from LEADS that is overdue
or due today, the late ones on top marked **Late**. Then overdue CALENDAR
entries with **Move to today**, then today's entries in order, then releases.

Click an entry to open it: its attachments (the set, the stems folder, a link)
each open in one click, its checklist ticks off in place, and **An hour later**
or **Tomorrow** moves it on. The tick on the left marks it done.

**Add** files a new CALENDAR entry: what, its kind, a time or the whole day,
today or tomorrow, and anything dropped on it as attachments. **Open CALENDAR**
opens the register in the console.

## Follow-ups on the strip

A follow-up on the strip can be dealt with right there. Tick its square to mark
it done without logging anything, or click it to open it:

![strip-06-follow-up.png](strip-06-follow-up.png)

| Press                                                  | And                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Log and complete**                                   | Type what happened and its points, then **Log and complete**. It's logged on the lead and the follow-up is done |
| **Open the lead**                                      | The lead (or the opportunity) opens in LEADS                                                                    |
| **In an hour**, **This evening**, **Tomorrow morning** | Snoozes its reminder until then. It stays overdue                                                               |
| **Dismiss**                                            | No more reminders for it. It stays open                                                                         |

Snooze and Dismiss are offered once a follow-up is late.

![strip-07-log.png](strip-07-log.png)

Logging from the strip works as it does on the lead: the suggestions are
REGULATION's predefined interactions, picking one fills in its points, and
typing a suggestion's name in full brings its points too. **Back** returns
without logging.

See the LEADS chapter for what follow-ups are and how temperature works.

## Reminders

**CALENDAR's entries**: a Windows notification comes a few minutes before a
timed entry starts (10 by default; REGULATION sets it, or turns it off). It
says what, when, and what it opens. Click it and the entry opens on the strip,
its attachments one click away.

**Follow-ups**: when Haven opens, one notification says what is overdue; after
that, each follow-up reminds you as it comes due. Click one and it opens on the
strip with **Log and complete**, **Snooze** and **Dismiss**, or at the lead in
the console while the console is on screen. REGULATION, under LEADS, turns
these on and off.

## New project

![strip-11-temporary.png](strip-11-temporary.png)

The strip's **New project** opens the vestibule's form beside it, with
**FILED** or **TEMPORARY** at the top.

- **FILED** (the default): a name, the shelf it's filed on, a colour.
  **Create** files it, copies your template set into it and opens it in
  Ableton.
- **TEMPORARY**: only a name, and that's optional. **Open in Live** copies your
  template set into TEMPORARY PROJECTS and opens it, with nothing filed. It
  goes to the Recycle Bin a day after it was last saved. See the ARCHIVE
  chapter, under Temporary projects.

## Recent projects

![strip-12-recent.png](strip-12-recent.png)

The **Recent projects** pin, the clock, holds the last twenty sets opened: the
same list as ARCHIVE's RECENT, the way Live's **Open Recent Set** lists them.

1. Rest the pointer on it for a moment, or press it. The sets drop down from
   the pin.
2. Press a set to open it in Ableton.
3. **Dossier** opens a project's dossier in the console. **All recent
   projects** opens ARCHIVE on its RECENT lens.

Dropped down by the pointer, the list doesn't take the keyboard from Live, and
it goes once the pointer has left the pin and the list. Pressed for, it stays
until you press `Escape` or somewhere else.

A strip from before 1.27.0 is given the pin once, first, or just after New
project. Move it or take it off like any other pin; taken off, it stays off.

## The More menu

The **⋯** button holds what the strip doesn't show:

- **Open a website…**
- **Open the console**
- **Open the vestibule**
- **Strip settings**: REGULATION, at QUICK STRIP
- **Hide the strip**: switches it off until you bring it back
- **Quit Candy Haven**

The gear beside it opens REGULATION at the strip's settings directly.

## Quitting Candy Haven

The power button at the end of the strip's tools quits Candy Haven: the
console, the strip, the archive and the broadcast kit, all of it. It is on the
strip in every look, and it can't be moved or taken off.

![strip-08-quit.png](strip-08-quit.png)

1. Press it once. It turns crimson and says **Press again to quit Candy
   Haven**.
2. Press it again within three seconds to quit.

If you don't, it settles back after three seconds and nothing happens. The
second press is there because the strip is always on screen, and one stray
click shouldn't take the archive down.

> Quitting is not hiding. **Hide the strip** only takes the strip off screen;
> Haven keeps running, with the archive up and the overlays serving.
> **Quit Candy Haven** on the tray icon and in the More menu quit too, in one
> press.

## At launch

REGULATION, under STARTUP, says what opens when Haven starts:

| Choice        | Opens                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `QUICK STRIP` | The strip alone. The default                         |
| `VESTIBULE`   | The vestibule as well: a new project, or the console |
| `CONSOLE`     | The console as well                                  |

![regulation-07-startup.png](regulation-07-startup.png)

The strip itself opens at every launch while it's switched on, the sign-in
launch included. **Hide the strip** in its More menu switches it off; **Show the
Quick Strip** on the tray icon, or **Show the strip** in REGULATION, brings it
back.

## Settings

REGULATION's QUICK STRIP category holds everything above: whether it shows, its
look, whether it stays on top of other windows, whether Today is on it, how
early CALENDAR's reminders come, where it sits, and every pin and group.
