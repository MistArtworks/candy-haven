# LORE

The lore of Nayara, written here and published to the website: its chapters,
and the planets they are read beside. Behind the DISPATCH sign-in.

![lore-01-chapters.png](lore-01-chapters.png)

## Written here, published there

Everything written in LORE is kept on this PC, in the archive: the chapters,
the planet library and their order. **File changes** saves it here and nowhere
else, so saving works with the network down, and the other copy of the console
never sees what has not been published.

The website shows a chapter only once it is **published**. Publishing sends that
one chapter as it stands, with a copy of its planet. Until the first chapter is
published, the website reads the lore from its own files; the first publish
switches it over for good, and from then on it shows only what is published
from here. The page says **Not live yet** while the website has not switched.

| Status      | Means                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DRAFT`     | Only on this PC; the website has never had it           |
| `PUBLISHED` | On the website, exactly as it is here                   |
| `CHANGED`   | On the website, but changed here since it was published |
| `UNSAVED`   | Typed in WRITE and not filed yet                        |

## The three tabs

- **CHAPTERS**: every chapter in its order, with where each stands and the
  planet it is read beside. Click a chapter, or its **Write** button, to open it.
- **WRITE**: one chapter, given the whole page.
- **PLANETS**: the planet library, and the planet being made.

## Three ways to see the chapters

The switch at the top of CHAPTERS lays them out three ways. The one chosen is
kept for next time.

| Layout  | Shows                                                                        |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `LIST`  | A row each, with a small planet. The one layout where chapters are reordered |
| `CARDS` | A card each, with its planet large, its line, and where it stands            |
| `ORBIT` | The chapters round a ring, as the website's lore page sets them              |

![lore-14-cards.png](lore-14-cards.png)

ORBIT puts each chapter's planet on the ring, from the top and clockwise, with a
place kept for the chapter still to come, as the website does. The one in hand
is drawn large in the middle, moving, with its title, line, status and planet
beside it. Pick a planet on the ring to bring its chapter to the middle, or walk
the ring with the arrow keys; **Write** opens it, and **Open planet** opens its
planet in PLANETS. The numerals are gold for a published chapter, crimson for
one changed since, and faint for a draft.

![lore-15-orbit.png](lore-15-orbit.png)

## How to write a chapter

1. Open **CHAPTERS** and press **New chapter**.
2. Type its title and press **Create**. The chapter opens in WRITE as a draft.

   ![lore-02-new-chapter.png](lore-02-new-chapter.png)

3. Fill in **One line**: it is shown under the title, and along the orbit on
   the website.
4. Check the **Address**, the end of its link (`candy-heist.com/lore/...`). It is
   made from the title; **Match the title** makes it again after a rename.
5. Under **Planet**, press **Change** and pick one of your planets or a preset.

   ![lore-03-planet-picker.png](lore-03-planet-picker.png)

6. Write the text in the box. Use the bar above it as in a word processor (see
   the table below).
7. Press **File changes** in the bar at the bottom of the window. The chapter is
   saved on this PC, still a draft.

![lore-04-write.png](lore-04-write.png)

What is typed and not filed yet is also kept in the window's storage. If the
console closes first, the chapter opens with it next time, marked **Restored**.

## The formatting bar

![lore-05-format-bar.png](lore-05-format-bar.png)

Select some words and press a button, or press it first and type. Hover over a
button to see what it does.

| Button           | Shortcut | What it writes     | What the website shows           |
| ---------------- | -------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **B**            | `Ctrl+B` | `**words**`        | Bold                             |
| _I_              | `Ctrl+I` | `*words*`          | Italic, in the website's gold    |
| Heading          |          | `## A heading`     | A heading inside the chapter     |
| Bulleted list    |          | `- item`           | A list                           |
| Terms            |          | `- **Term**: text` | A term picked out, then its text |
| Large quote      |          | `> a line`         | A line set large, on its own     |
| Clear formatting |          |                    | Plain text again                 |

- Each button turns its formatting off again on text that already has it.
- **Enter** starts a new paragraph. In a list it starts the next item; on an
  empty item it ends the list. **Shift+Enter** breaks the line without starting
  a paragraph.
- **Undo** and **Redo**, or `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y`, step through the changes.
- The marks the bar writes stay visible in the text, so they can be typed by
  hand too. Anything the website cannot show is pointed out under the box.

The count at the end of the bar is words, then characters out of 100,000.

## How to review a chapter before publishing

1. In WRITE, use **View** at the top right:
   - **Write**: the text alone, at a comfortable width.
   - **Side by side**: the text beside the chapter as the website will show it.
   - **Preview**: the chapter alone, as the website will show it.
2. Read it in **Preview**. The preview is drawn by the same reader the website
   uses, with the chapter's planet, numeral, title and one line.
3. Check the status beside the chapter's name at the top, and the line above the
   buttons at the bottom: when it was saved on this PC, and whether and when it
   was published.

![lore-06-preview.png](lore-06-preview.png)

The view you chose is remembered for next time. **Chapter**, at the top left,
switches to another chapter without going back to CHAPTERS.

## How to publish

1. Open the chapter in WRITE.
2. Press **Publish**. With anything unsaved it reads **Save and publish**, and
   files it first. A chapter needs a title, one line, some text, an address and
   a planet; until it has them, the button says which is missing.
3. The first time anything is published, the console asks first, because it
   switches the website over from its own files. Press **Publish** to go ahead.

   ![lore-07-publish-warning.png](lore-07-publish-warning.png)

4. The status turns **PUBLISHED**, and the website shows the chapter.

![lore-08-published.png](lore-08-published.png)

A published chapter changed here reads **CHANGED**, and **Publish changes**
sends the new version. **Revert to published** drops the changes filed here and
goes back to what the website has.

**Unpublish** takes a chapter off the website and keeps it here as a draft.
**Delete** removes a draft from this PC; for a published chapter it reads
**Delete everywhere**, and takes it off the website too. Both ask first.

## How to change the order

1. In **CHAPTERS**, on **LIST**, drag a chapter by its grip, or hover over it and use the
   arrows at its end. The order is kept here.
2. When the website shows the published chapters in another order, the list says
   so. Press **Publish order** to send this one, or **Use the website's** to take
   the website's.

![lore-09-order.png](lore-09-order.png)

A newly published chapter goes last on the website until the order is
published.

## Two writers

You each write on your own PC, and see each other's chapters once they are
published: the page fetches what is published when it opens, and again with
**Check for new**. A chapter the other person published opens here as it is on
the website, and becomes a draft on this PC when a change to it is filed.

If the other person published a chapter after your draft started from it,
publishing asks rather than writing over theirs:

- **Publish mine over theirs** replaces their version on the website.
- **Take theirs instead** drops your draft and opens their version.
- **Cancel** keeps your draft as it is, unpublished, to compare first.

![lore-10-conflict.png](lore-10-conflict.png)

If they took it off the website, publishing asks whether to put it back.

## Planets

![lore-11-planets.png](lore-11-planets.png)

**PLANETS** is the library: your planets, then the eleven presets the lore began
with. A preset never changes; **Duplicate to edit** makes a planet of yours from
it. **New planet** starts from a bare planet or any preset.

A planet is a stack of layers over the planet itself (its surface, rim and a
faint shade). The left side shows it and its layers; the right side changes
whatever is picked.

## How to make a planet

1. In **PLANETS**, press **New planet**, name it, choose what it starts from
   (**Bare**, or a preset) and press **Make it**. Or open a preset and press
   **Duplicate to edit**.
2. Press **Add layer**. Layers come in five groups: Structure, Network, Marks,
   Interference, and Beyond the edge (rings and moons). Pick one and press
   **Add layer**, or double-click it.

   ![lore-12-add-layer.png](lore-12-add-layer.png)

3. The new layer is picked. On the right, its settings fold into sections:
   **Colour** and **Shape** are open; **Movement**, **Position and size**, and
   **Line and blend** open when you click them. A closed section says what it is
   set to.

   ![lore-13-layer-settings.png](lore-13-layer-settings.png)

4. Pick a colour from the website's palette, or any colour from the square at
   the end of the row. **Strength** sets how strongly it shows.
5. Shape shows its main settings first, and the rest under **More shape
   settings**. **Shuffle** gives the layer a fresh take on its random parts.
6. Under **Movement**, choose **Still**, **Spin**, **Pulse**, **Flicker** or
   **Drift**, and how long one turn takes. The website holds motion still for
   visitors who ask for less.
7. Pick **The planet itself**, below the layers, to paint its surface, rim and
   shade.
8. Press **File changes**.

Tips:

- The top of the layer list is drawn on top. Drag a layer to restack it. The eye
  hides a layer; the lock keeps it as it is, even through **Shuffle all**.
- **Shuffle all** gives every unlocked layer a fresh take. **Undo**, or `Ctrl+Z`
  outside a text field, steps back.
- **Pause**, over the preview, holds the motion still to look closely.
- Under the preview it says whether the planet is light or heavy for phones:
  past about 1,500 shapes, a phone may stutter as the planet scrolls by.

Planets are kept on this PC. A published chapter carries a copy of its planet,
so changing a planet changes nothing on the website until the chapters drawn
with it are published again; until then they read **CHANGED**. A planet a
chapter uses cannot be deleted.

## Which website

As with CONTACT: the installed console publishes to the live website, and a
development copy to the development server on the same machine.
REGULATION → INTEGRATIONS → **Website address** overrides both. What each
website has published is kept apart; the drafts here are the same whichever
website is set.
