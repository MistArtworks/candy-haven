# SERVICES

Bookings from the website: DJ enquiries now, producer work to come. Behind the
DISPATCH sign-in, and checked in with CONTACT's messages.

![services-01-enquiries.png](services-01-enquiries.png)

## DJ

The website's "Book me as a DJ" page files each enquiry with a reference such
as `DJ-4HM9TQ`. They arrive here exactly as CONTACT's messages do: copied when
the console starts or when you press **Check for new**, readable with the
network down, and gone from the page when nobody is signed in.

| Status      | Means                                  |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `NEW`       | Not yet taken up                       |
| `IN TALKS`  | Being discussed with whoever sent it   |
| `CONFIRMED` | Booked                                 |
| `DECLINED`  | Turned down                            |
| `ARCHIVED`  | Done with, and out of the default list |

There is no "read". Having looked at an enquiry is not an answer to it, so a
new one stays new, and keeps the rail's count lit, until it is taken up or
turned down.

## How to take up an enquiry

1. Open the **DJ** tab and press **Check for new**.
2. Pick an enquiry in the list. It opens beside the list: who is asking and in
   what role, the event, the venue, the budget, and what they wrote about it.
3. Press **Reply by email**. Your own mail opens with their address and the
   event filled in.
4. Set **Status** to **IN TALKS** while it is being discussed.
5. Keep track in **Note**: dates offered, fees, the set length. It is filed as
   you type.
6. Set **CONFIRMED** once it is booked, or **DECLINED** if it is not happening.
7. When nothing more will come of it, set **ARCHIVED**.

![services-02-enquiry.png](services-02-enquiry.png)

The list shows **OPEN** by default: new and in talks. **Show** narrows it to one
status or widens it to **ALL**; **Search** looks through the name, the address,
the event, the venue, what they wrote and the reference.

Statuses, notes and deleting work as they do in CONTACT: statuses and notes
are your own tracking and never leave this machine, and deleting needs the
website and removes the enquiry everywhere.

## Being told

New enquiries are counted beside SERVICES on the rail, on the **DJ** tab, and in
the notice the console gives when it starts. Clicking that notice opens SERVICES
when it has no messages to point at.
