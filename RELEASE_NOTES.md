The ARCHIVE is rebuilt, THE CONCORD stays on screen, NOW TRANSMITTING serves four sources, and there is a new department for the two of us to talk in.

## Before you update

- The ARCHIVE's database is wiped on first run. Nothing on disk is touched — every project folder, set and sample is exactly where it was — but stages, notes, tags and favourites are not carried across. The new tree is built by scanning again.
- TRANSMISSIONS is gone. A calendar over releases will be rebuilt on the new release records.

## ARCHIVE

- The department now makes the structure rather than discovering it. A filing root is chosen once, the app builds its own tree inside it, and a project is provisioned: a real folder, your own template .als renamed to match, and the six scaffold folders beside Ableton's Samples and Backup.
- Genres hold folders and projects. There is no sub-genre level.
- Albums, EPs and compilations are VOLUMES — metadata only, nothing created on disk. Membership lives on the project, so a track list cannot drift from the records it names.
- Six lenses over one register: ALL, GENRES, UNFILED, VOLUMES, RELEASES, BIN. UNFILED sits beside the browser, because filing is a drag from one to the other and both ends have to be visible.
- Deleting is two verbs that do not sound alike. FORGET drops the record; DELETE moves the folder to a recycle bin inside Candy Haven, so it stays retrievable.
- Colours you pick are stored exactly as picked. The old clamp folded any hue outside the house range into it, so a colour you chose came back a different one.
- The favourite mark is settable from the ledger and the board, not only from tiles.
- The dossier opens the project folder or the set in Ableton, from the foot of the analysis panel.

## DISPATCH

A new department, and the only one whose record is not on your machine. Feedback and suggestions between the two of us, held in a shared database: file an item, discuss it, and it is resolved or denied with a reason. Unread comments are marked on the card. Sign in with your own account; the database refuses everyone else.

## OBSERVATORY

- NOW TRANSMITTING serves its four presentations as four browser sources with four addresses, so a scene can be built around whichever shape fits its layout instead of choosing one for every scene at once.
- THE CONCORD holds its resting composition between polls rather than vanishing. A settled result returns to rest on its own once it has had its linger.
